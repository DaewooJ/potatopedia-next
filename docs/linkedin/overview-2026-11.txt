Potatopedia is the world's potato knowledge base: an independent, AI-powered reference for everyone who grows, trades, processes, stores or studies potatoes.

Ask any question and get a sourced answer drawn from 12,800+ verified data points across 780+ primary sources: FAOSTAT, USDA, CIP (International Potato Center), UN Comtrade, ICAR-CPRI, Europatat and peer-reviewed journals. Every figure carries its source and year, and when several estimates exist for one year the latest official one wins.

What you will find on potatopedia.com
• Country profiles with production, area and yield data for 204 countries
• Trade flows for fresh, seed, frozen fries, chips, flakes and starch
• A Varieties Atlas with traits for 244 commercial varieties and a finder that matches variety to use and climate
• A directory of potato processors, seed companies and machinery makers, built from official sources only
• Guides on seed systems, storage, diseases and pests, processing quality and machinery
• Market prices, industry events and the Potatopedia Journal of weekly data-led posts
• Country reports in PDF and a media kit for advertisers

Who it is for
Farmers and growers, seed companies, processors, exporters and importers, cold-storage operators, equipment makers, researchers, students and journalists.

Follow this page for one data-led post every weekday: a country, a variety, a company or a market in numbers, always sourced.
