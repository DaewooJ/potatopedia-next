The rules we keep

  • Clearly labelled sponsors. Never paid rankings. Never paid facts.
  • Every paid item says Sponsored, Published with us or Advertisement.
  • Sponsors never touch news items or neutral pages.
  • Launch partners: before a title reaches 1,000 confirmed subscribers (or 3 issues at a 35% unique open rate) a few well-known companies are invited to a free, labelled slot.
