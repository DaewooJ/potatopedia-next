# World Potato Yearbook 2027: plan (13 Nov 2026)

Plan only. Nothing is built or published. One yearbook per year; the first edition is the 2027 edition, early 2027. It is the year's biggest sponsor sale (prompt section 2). Neutral and global: no country is given priority, India is one market among many.

## What it is
A reference book (PDF/flipbook plus web pages) with the world potato year in sourced numbers: production, trade, processing, seed, prices, varieties, and the industry's people and events. Every table and chart names its primary source, table year and release (latest government estimate wins: Final > 3rd Advance > 2nd > 1st, and the release is named). No figure is typed in from memory; tables are generated from the sources by script, with each source URL and retrieval date in an appendix.

## Outline
**Front matter**: editor's letter (signed, editor TBD), how to read this book, method and sources, labelling rules ("Clearly labelled sponsors. Never paid rankings. Never paid facts.").

**Part 1: The world in numbers**
1. Production, area and yield by country and region (FAOSTAT; table year stated, e.g. most recent complete year).
2. Trade in potatoes by HS code: 0701 fresh, 0710.10 frozen, 2004.10 frozen fries, 2005.20 chips, 1105 flour/flakes, 1108.13 starch (UN Comtrade; Eurostat for the EU).
3. Prices: export unit values from trade data (not retail shelf prices); wholesale and contract references from published primary sources only.

**Part 2: Regions** (one chapter each, same template: production, trade, seed system, processing, climate and policy, who to know)
4. Europe (EU, UK, Eastern Europe; Eurostat, NEPG/Europatat statistics, national bodies)
5. North America (USDA NASS, ERS, FAS; Statistics Canada; Potatoes USA, Potato Growers of Alberta)
6. Latin America (FAOSTAT; national institutes; CIP)
7. Africa (FAOSTAT; CIP; national seed bodies)
8. Asia-Pacific (FAOSTAT; national statistics; India as one section among China, Bangladesh, Australia, New Zealand, others)

**Part 3: Segments** (one chapter each)
9. Frozen fries and processing
10. Chips and snacks
11. Flakes, dehydrated and starch
12. Fresh and table potatoes
13. Seed potatoes: certification, trade rules, varieties and registrations
14. Storage and sprout control
15. Disease and pests (PVY, late blight, wireworm, zebra chip, cyst nematodes): the year's notable findings, from peer-reviewed sources

**Part 4: Directory and calendar**
16. Companies (from the verified directory, official sources only; sponsored listings labelled)
17. Events of the year (from the Exhibitions record)
18. Contributors' essays (from the /contribute programme, bylined; see `contributors.md`)

**Appendix**: data tables as CSV, source list with URLs and retrieval dates, glossary, corrections log.

## Data sources (all primary)
FAOSTAT (FAO); UN Comtrade; USDA NASS, ERS and FAS; Statistics Canada; Eurostat; CIP (International Potato Center); national potato boards and seed certification bodies; Europatat and NEPG statistics; peer-reviewed journals (American Journal of Potato Research, Potato Research, Potato Journal). Banned, never cited or linked: PotatoPro, PotatoNewsToday, FreshPlaza, TimesCA, indianpotato.com and other aggregators.
