# Target contributors for /contribute (30 names)

Prepared 13 Nov 2026 for Devendra. **Show-only list: no outreach has been made and none is drafted.** Machine-readable copy: `contributors.csv`.

## How the list was made

- Names came from authors cited in the backend corpus (`~/Projects/potatopedia-backend/data/*.txt`) and from institution staff pages for each potato topic. The source list `config/news_sources.yaml` did not exist yet in the backend on 13 Nov 2026, so it was not used.
- Every person was checked on an institution or association page. Contact route = the public profile URL in the table only. No emails were collected, nothing was scraped, no LinkedIn-only people are included.
- **Verification level A** = the person's own institution or association page was fetched on 13 Nov 2026 and shows the role. **Level B** = the role was confirmed from an institution or association page dated as shown, or read through a search excerpt because the page itself blocked direct fetching; re-check the page before any approach. The `verification_evidence` column in the CSV says which.
- "Topic they could write" and "why" are our proposals from the person's published field, not anything they have agreed to.
