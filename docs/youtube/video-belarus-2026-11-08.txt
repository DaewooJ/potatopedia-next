Belarus eats 166.9 kg of potatoes per person a year, more than any country | Potatopedia data story

DESCRIPTION
Belarus eats more potatoes per person than any other country: 166.9 kg a year in 2023, the highest of 177 countries and almost five times the world figure of 33.7 kg.
In 71 seconds: what that number measures, how far Belarus leads Ukraine, how consumption has moved since 2010, and where the rest of a shrinking harvest goes.
