Potatopedia is the world's potato knowledge base: verified production, trade, variety, seed and processing data for 204 countries, at www.potatopedia.com.

On this channel we publish short data stories about potatoes worldwide: production and consumption, trade flows, prices, varieties, seed potatoes, french fries and processing. Each video answers one question with figures you can check. Every number on screen carries its source and year.

Our sources are FAOSTAT, USDA, CIP (International Potato Center), UN Comtrade, ICAR-CPRI, Europatat and peer-reviewed research. When several estimates exist for one year, the latest official one is used.

The videos are made for growers, processors, traders, seed companies, machinery makers, researchers, students and anyone who follows agriculture and food security.
